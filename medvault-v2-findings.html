Architecture Transformations & Remediation Log

A chronological phase log of the 42 remediations applied to elevate the system to production grade.

Phase 0: Legacy Cleanup
Deleted 3 zero-access-control legacy contracts (patientcontract.sol, doctorcontract.sol, auditcontract.sol). Fixed critical admin() → owner() bug and useEffect event listener leak.
Phase 1: IdentityRegistryV2
Implemented 1-slot packed storage, monotonic uint32 IDs. Transitioned to non-upgradeable architecture (removed proxy overhead saving ~2,600 gas per tx).
Phase 2: ConsentLedgerV2
Engineered packed consent word and composite uint64 mapping keys. Introduced batch grant/revoke APIs for multi-provider onboarding.
Phase 3: RecordRegistryV2
Deployed 3-slot RecordNode architecture utilizing the head pointer pattern. Eradicated timestamp storage in favor of pure EVM events.
Phase 4: Optimization Engine
Configured dual-mode deployment scripts (prod-direct / dev-upgradeable). Enabled Hardhat Solidity optimizer at runs: 200 (upgradeable to 1000).
Phase 5: Client-Side Engine
Refactored frontend to abandon O(n) RPC scans in favor of event-indexed reads. Removed double encryption, isolated per-record password state, and enforced wallet-signed localStorage encryption.
Phase 6 & 7: Verification & L2
Finalized unit, invariant, and gas snapshot CI assertions. Executed Polygon L2 migration and empirical fee estimation modeling.
